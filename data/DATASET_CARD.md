# Dataset and disclosure card

My 2022 dissertation investigated mobile-navigation acceptance among surveyed drivers in Sri Lanka. This repository provides **historical aggregate results**, not an open survey dataset.

**Original research:** *An Explorative Study on Driver Acceptance of Mobile Navigation Systems to Mitigate Road Accidents in Sri Lanka*, BSc (Hons) Business Information Systems, Cardiff Metropolitan University, submitted 12 June 2022.

**What was measured:** nine explanatory questionnaire constructs and one acceptance outcome, using five-point responses. The analysed SPSS sample is **311 cases**. The dissertation refers to both 325 and 326 initial responses; that inconsistency has not been silently corrected.

**Public tables:** correlations, Cronbach's alpha, KMO and PCA first-component variance, simple regressions and multiple-regression coefficients, **as already reported in the assessed dissertation**. These are transcriptions, not raw responses and not results calculated from the repository's fabricated example.

**Not released:** personally identifying metadata, original respondent rows, questionnaire exports, SPSS SAV/SPS/SPV, any unredacted appendix, and new private-source-derived statistical audits. Privacy, reuse rights and participant expectations are distinct from whether a code test passes.

**Appropriate uses:** inspecting the original dissertation's aggregate evidence, understanding the interpretation of statistical models and running the separately labelled synthetic examples.

**Not appropriate:** recomputing the original SPSS model from aggregate data, inferring the answers of individuals or subgroups, calculating collision reduction, establishing a representative rate for all Sri Lankan drivers, or treating the public synthetic example as an empirical replication.

**Source boundaries:** Original dissertation tables are the authority for the historical numerical reporting. The later Python work is independently written methodological teaching code. For exact unresolved limitations, see [Evidence and limitations](../docs/EVIDENCE_AND_LIMITATIONS.md).
