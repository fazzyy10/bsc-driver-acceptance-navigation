# The research I would do next

I would not start by collecting a bigger dataset without first deciding what the new study should establish.

**First, measurement.** Trust, satisfaction and perceived usefulness sound distinct, but respondents can experience them together. I would inspect item wording, construct overlap, collinearity and the stability of scale definitions.

**Second, sampling.** A different city, age range, level of digital familiarity or driving experience may produce different answers. Any new sampling plan needs to say in advance which drivers it is meant to describe.

**Third, real behaviour.** Acceptance is one thing, use is another. A prospective, ethically collected study of actual use is required to connect perceptions to behaviour.

**Fourth, safety.** Even using the app does not automatically cause fewer accidents. Measuring a road-safety effect would require much stronger evidence, properly chosen comparison groups, exposure measures and control of confounding.

**Finally, more defensible analysis.** I would predefine testing thresholds, check uncertainty and sensitivity, describe missing-data decisions, and avoid interpreting a fitted-sample R² as future predictive performance.

These are **proposals**, not experiments completed by the 2022 dissertation or this public repository.
